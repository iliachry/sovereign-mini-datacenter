# 19-Inch 9U Ruggedized Enclosure Manufacturing Guide

## 1. Sheet Metal & Framing Materials

* **Chassis Outer Panels:** 2.0mm Anodized 6061-T6 Aluminum Sheet Metal.
* **Internal Rack Rails:** 2.0mm Heavy-Duty Cold Rolled Steel 19" Mounting Rails (Tapped M6 holes).
* **Corner Guards & Edging:** Heavy-duty vulcanized rubber corners and extruded aluminum tongue-and-groove edging.

---

## 2. Fabrication Steps

1. **Laser Cutting / CNC Punching:**
   * Cut 2.0mm aluminum side, top, and bottom panels per `cad/rack_enclosure.scad` coordinates.
   * Cut rear fan & liquid pass-through ports (2x 360mm radiator fan cutouts).
2. **Bending & Forming:**
   * Bend side panels on a CNC press brake (90° bend radius = 2.0mm).
3. **Assembly & Riveting:**
   * Fasten corner extrusions using 4.8mm stainless steel blind pop rivets.
   * Mount 19" rack rails at 482.6mm center-to-center spacing.
